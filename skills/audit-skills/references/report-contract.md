## Presentation traps

Earlier reports became unreliable when they handed verification back, sampled convenient
references, graded descriptions by vibe, skipped active instructions or cross-home
overlap, inferred utility from aggregate session totals, accepted free-form worker prose,
or mixed reporting with edits. Those failures motivated the controller's Iron Law and
structured phases.
